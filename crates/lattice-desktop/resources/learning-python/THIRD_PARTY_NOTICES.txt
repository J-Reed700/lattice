Build-only dependencies (not included as executables in the runtime bundle)

WASI SDK 24.0 is used to compile CPython to wasm32-wasip1. Its source and
license details are at https://github.com/WebAssembly/wasi-sdk. The pinned
release archive and SHA-256 are in PROVENANCE.json.

Wasmtime 48.0.1 CLI is used by CPython's upstream WASI build helper. It is
not shipped in the runtime bundle. Its license is Apache-2.0 OR MIT; the
pinned archive and SHA-256 are in PROVENANCE.json. Lattice's in-process
Wasmtime Rust crates are declared and locked by Cargo separately.
