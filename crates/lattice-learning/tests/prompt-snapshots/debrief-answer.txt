- Clarity [c3]: Said what they were doing.

A hint: hints holds the names of the hints it cites. Cite only what is in the session; the app checks every item and drops one that is wrong.

Credit steps the learner took and described; do not claim a step was missing that the session shows. Time per stage, checks, hints and settings are shown beside your scores; use them as context and do not restate them.

Each row gets feedback about what this session demonstrated, and one next step: a concrete thing to do next time. Write for the learner, in the second person.

Answer with an entries list, one entry for each criterion listed above. In an entry, of is the criterion's name as listed, without its brackets; score is a number of points or insufficient_evidence; quotes, parts, checks, cases and hints each hold the names of the things of that kind the row cites, and are empty lists when it cites none; feedback and nextStep are your own words.
